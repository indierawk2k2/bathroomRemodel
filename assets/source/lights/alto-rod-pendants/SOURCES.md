## Verified vs assumed

- Verified: 6" globe, 5.75" canopy, the six stock lengths, E26 / 60 W, damp
  rating, Brass finish.
- **The lengths are overall** (canopy top to globe bottom), per the drawing's
  "Overall Fixture Length" dimension, not rod lengths. So the longest stock
  length (43.75") puts the globe bottom at 76.25" and its centre at 79.25"
  from a 120" ceiling, 9.25" above the mirror's widest point (70" at the
  default mirror). Centring the globe on the mirror needs a custom ~53"
  length (Cedar & Moss make custom lengths on request; not priced).
- From the photo / drawing (measured, approximate): brass cap over the top
  ~1.65" of the globe; 0.7" collar on the globe, 0.6" collar under the
  canopy; rod ~3/8"; canopy a shallow dish ~0.9" deep.
- Assumed: the opal glass fully hides the bulb (it is a closed globe whose
  only opening is under the brass cap).
