## Specs (from the pages)

- Lighting Originals: width **4.75"**, height **23.75"**,
  extension **6"**, backplate width 4.75", "shade top width" 4.5"; 1 x
  **G9**, 6 W (LED), not included; glass "**Nuage etched inside**" (clear
  globes etched inside to a speckled opal); steel; aged brass; UL / ETL,
  **damp rated**; 120 V.
- HVL: width 4.75", height 23.75", extension 6", **top to centre 10.25"**,
  2.5 lb; backplate **round, 4.75" diam.**; 1 x G9 LED, 6 W max; **CETL
  damp**; "clear glass globes etched on the inside creating a speckled
  opal glass interior" on "slender, curved arms".
- Design (photo): a long flat bar on the round two-step plate (held by a
  square clip). It runs from well below the plate up past it and curves
  forward over the top into a hook that holds the globe just in front of
  the bar.

## Verified vs assumed

- Verified: 4.75" W x 23.75" H x 6" extension, 10.25" top to plate
  centre, 4.75" round plate, G9, damp, enclosed etched globe.
- From the studio photo (45 px/in): globe **4.5"** diam. (matching the
  4.5" "shade top width"), with its centre 3.9" below the top. The bar is
  ~0.5" wide.
- Assumed: bar thickness 0.22", plate depth 0.55" in two steps, bar 0.3"
  off the plate, and the hook's curve radius. The hook ends over the globe
  at the 6" extension, so the globe axis is 3.75" off the wall.

## Placement

Globe centre on the mirror's widest point (70" at the default mirror):
globe 67.75-72.25", plate centre 63.65", bar 50.2-73.9". For every
"Mirror bottom" default (centre 64-84") the bar stays above 44". At the
lowest sconce setting (centre 56") it would reach down to 36.2", below the
40" wainscot top. On the west strip the bar's lower end passes in front of
the existing towel ring (preset 3 shot).
