## Specs (from the page)

- Specs block: width 16.9", height 45.5", length 16.9"; min. hanging length
  **19.2"**, max. hanging length **44.2"**; shade width **17.1"**, shade
  height **5.1"**; canopy **5"** dia. Materials: metal, rattan, glass.
- Description: natural rattan shade; **glass diffuser**; **brass finish
  hardware** ("vintaged brass-colored metal"); adjustable height; can hang

## Verified vs assumed

- Verified (page): the 17.1" x 5.1" shade, the 19.2–44.2" hanging length,
  the 5" canopy, the brass-finish hardware and the glass diffuser. The
  drawing gives a 7.3" shade including its woven collar and rod sections of
  6 + 6 + 12 + 12".
- Measured off the detail photo, so approximate: the diffuser bowl is
  about 8" across and its bottom hangs about 2.6" below the rattan rim; the
  collar is about 5" across and 2.2" tall.
- The rod sections do not add up exactly to the stated range, so the hang
  is modelled as continuous within 19.2–44.2", with a joint drawn at each
  section from the shade up.
- Assumed: the hanging length runs from the ceiling to the fixture's lowest
  point.
- The weave uses the project's `rattan` texture (photo 00, the Adara
  chandelier; see docs/textures.md), not this product's own weave.
