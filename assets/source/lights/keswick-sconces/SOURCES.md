## Verified vs assumed

- Verified: 5" W x 11" H x 8.75" extension, 5.25" top to plate centre,
  4.75" x 6" x 0.75" plate, bell with a 2" top opening, 5" bottom and 5"
  height, A19 E26, dimmable, damp.
- From the studio photo (47 px/in): the bell's shoulder is ~3.4" wide,
  with near-straight walls flaring to the rolled 5" rim. The socket holder
  is ~1.9" diam. with rings, a knurled fitter, a swivel ball and a thumb
  knob. The plate has two round bosses, and the gooseneck rises from the
  upper one.
- Assumed: gooseneck tube 0.4" diam. and its half-circle radius. The
  radius follows from the 11" height and 8.75" extension, which put the
  shade axis 6.25" off the wall.

## Orientation and bulb (brief)

The maker allows shade up or down; it is modelled **shade down**. A
standard A19 in this 5" bell would end only ~1.7" above the rim, so the
model recesses the bulb's glass to end **2.1" above the rim**. With the
shade at 67.5-72.5", the rim hides the bulb from standing eye level
(60-68"): the preset 1 and 3 shots show only the opal bell. In the real
fixture, a short A19 or an A15 frosted LED keeps the bulb inside the bell.
