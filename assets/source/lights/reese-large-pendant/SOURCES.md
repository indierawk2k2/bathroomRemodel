## Specs (read from the page's SPECIFICATIONS block)

- Width / diameter **14"**, height **17.25"**, weight 6.89 lb.
- Hang type: 120" fabric-covered cord (black fabric cord). Minimum height
  **21.5"**, maximum height **143"**. Sloped-ceiling compatible.
- Canopy: round, **4.75"** diam. x **0.5"** deep.
- Shade: glass, **opal glossy**, 14" x 17.25".
- Electrical: **cUL damp**. 1 x **A19 E26** medium base, 60 W max, 120 V.

## Verified vs assumed

- Verified (page): the 14" diameter, the 17.25" overall body height, the
  21.5–143" cord range, the 4.75" x 0.5" canopy, the opal glossy glass, the
  damp rating and the A19 E26 bulb.
- Measured off the studio photo (42.5 px per inch from the 14" width), so
  these are approximate. The widest point is about 6.8" above the bottom.
  The globe narrows to about 7.3" under the brass trumpet cap (the cap is
  about 6.3" tall, with a neck of about 0.6") and to about 11" at the flat
  brass ring (about 12.3" across, about 2.45" above the bottom). The lower
  opal dome is about 9.2" across and about 2.4" deep.
- Assumed: the bulb position (about 5.8" up). The "second shade beneath
  that conceals the bulb" is the lower opal dome, so the bulb is fully
  enclosed in opal glass and is never visible.
