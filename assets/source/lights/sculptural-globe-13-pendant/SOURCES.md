## Verified vs assumed

- Verified (page): all the dimensions above, the Antique Brass finish, the
  damp rating and the E26 socket.
- From the milk studio photo, so approximate: a near-spherical globe with
  a short neck (about 2.1" across) under a brass socket cup (about 1.6" x
  1.3"); a thicker sleeve (about 7") above the cup on a thin rigid stem;
  and a round bottom opening about 3.4" across.
- Assumed: the bulb sits at the globe centre, about 6" above the opening,
  so it can only be seen from nearly straight below.
- The page's figures replace the brief's assumed values (13" x ~11.5" with
  a brass-tone finish): the shade is 12.9" tall and the finish is Antique
  Brass.
