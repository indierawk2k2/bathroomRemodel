## From the photo, and what was assumed

- A full-height U-channel 4.25" wide behind the glass, its flanges reaching
  forward to about the glass axis; two bullet-shaped brass caps (~3.9" each)
  meet at the centre with a small gap; one cylinder rises from the upper cap
  and one hangs from the lower (the Harlan layout). The cylinders are open
  at their outer ends.
- The mount in the photo is a **narrow tall bar (~1.25" x 8")**, which does
  not match the page's "4.25" square" backplate; the model follows the
  photo. Not verified.
- Projection assumed: mount 1" deep, glass axis ~2.95" and glass front
  ~4.45" off the wall.
- Bulb hiding: the T10 bulbs sit in the caps and end ~3" short of each open
  end, so at the default height (fixture 58.1"-81.9") a standing eye (60-68")
  cannot see into either end. A seated person or a child looking up into the
  lower end could see the frosted bulb tip deep inside. The bulbs are not
  modelled.
