## Specs (from the page)

- Height **15.4"**. The spec list gives no width or depth. 1.8 kg per
  sconce; package 17 x 13 x 6.5".
- **Acid-washed frosted glass** cylinders; metal frame, brass colour;
  **up and down light**; 2 x **E26** per sconce (4 sockets in the set),
  60 W max total; 110 V; **dimmable** with a compatible dimmer; bulbs not
  included (A15, G45, corn bulbs recommended); ETL.
- Rating: "**Dry** environment use recommended". There is **no damp
  rating**, so the fixture is not rated for a bathroom with a shower. The
  option's description says so.

## Verified vs assumed

- The brief expected the width to be unlisted and to need an estimate of
  4.5-5". The page's **dimension drawing** gives it, so nothing was
  estimated: tube diameter **3.1"**, round backplate **4.7"** (so the
  overall width is 4.7"), extension **4.1"**. The tube is **15.4"** long
  on the room side and **12"** on the wall side, with both ends open and
  cut on a slant.
- Assumed: backplate depth 0.85" (the photo shows a deep drum), a central
  twin-socket holder (hidden by the frosted glass), and bulbs 3.7" above
  and below the centre (the two glow spots in the lit photo).
- The glass is treated as frosted and translucent, with no bulb visible,
  as in `claxy-rod-pendants.js`. The slanted ends open towards the wall,
  so from in front at standing eye level the glass lip hides the inside.
