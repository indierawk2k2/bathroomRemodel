## Specs (from the page and the dimension drawing)

- One variant on this page (brass with frosted glass). "Other Variations"
  links to separate products: Modern Brass Kitchen Pendant Light Rod Hanging
  Light Fixture; Modern Brass Kitchen Pendant Light Adjustable Hanging Light;
  Farmhouse Glass Kitchen Pendant Light Frosted Glass Shade; Industrial mini
  Glass Kitchen Pendant Light Clear Glass Shade; Modern Glass Kitchen Pendant
  Light Dimmable Light with Blue Finish; Modern Glass Kitchen Pendant Light
  Clear Glass and White Metal Shades.
- Finish: "electrophoretic brass" (brushed). Open **frosted glass cylinder**
  shade encircled by a "floating" brass band.
- Shade: **7"** wide (the band) x **6.3"** tall. From the studio photo the
  glass is about 5.8" diam. and the band 2.7" tall, starting 1.55" below the
  glass top.
- Canopy: **4.9"** diam. (about 1" tall, two knurled cap nuts, a swivel
  adaptor below it for sloped / vaulted ceilings).
- Rods: **three 12" rods and one 6" rod**; "Rod length is adjustable up to
  44.3 inches"; drawing: "Mini H 8.4"" (canopy top to shade bottom with no
  rods). Real overall drops are therefore 8.4" + {0, 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36,
  42}" = 8.4"–50.4" (44.3" = the 42" of rods plus ~2.3" of adaptor/stub).
- One **E26** socket, max 60 W, LED / incandescent / CFL (not included),
  downlight, dimmable, 120 V, hardwired, ETL. A reviewer notes that a
  standard large bulb shows below the glass rim (use a narrow bulb).
- Damp rating: **not stated**. The page lists Bathroom as a location, but its
  note says to use it in a dry environment for a longer service life.
