## Specs (from the pages)

- Mitzi page: height **29.5"**, width **4.75"**, shade height **15.75"**,
  shade diameter **4.0"**, backplate **oval 4.75" W x 7.5" H**; steel;
  **opal glossy glass**; 1 x **E26** medium base, 6 W (LED) each, bulb not
  included; **cUL damp**; "Hang only as pictured"; 5.0 lb (AGB).
- HVL spec page: width 4.75", height 29.5", **extension 5.75"**, top to
  centre 4", backplate oval 4.75" x 7.5", shade top / bottom 4", shade
  height 15.75", 5 lb.
- Design (photo): an elongated opal capsule, fully enclosed, threaded on a
  slender vertical brass rod that continues above the glass to a rounded
  finial beside the top of the backplate, and below it to a short finial.
  A short horizontal arm from the plate holds the rod through a collar.

## Verified vs assumed

- Verified from the pages: 29.5" overall height, 4.75" width, 4" x 15.75"
  glass, 4.75" x 7.5" oval plate, 5.75" extension (to the glass front; the
  glass axis is modelled 3.75" off the wall), 1 x E26, cUL damp.
- From the studio photo (37 px/in, 3/4 view): rod above the glass 8.9",
  below it 4.85" (8.9 + 15.75 + 4.85 = 29.5"), rod ~0.55" diam., plate top
  0.8" below the finial, arm ~4.4" below the top. HVL's "top to centre 4""
  would put the plate's top 0.25" above the finial, which the photo does
  not show. The model follows the photo (plate centre 4.55" below the top).
- Assumed: plate depth 0.6" and arm diameter 0.44". The rod is not drawn
  inside the glass, because the opal hides it.

## Placement check (brief)

Glass centre on the mirror's widest point (70" at the default mirror):
glass 62.1-77.9", whole fixture 57.3-86.8" (finial). The sconce slider
(56-84") sets the glass centre, and every "Mirror bottom" position
(36-56", so a centre of 64-84") stays inside it, so **no clamp is
needed**. At the top of that range the finial reaches 100.8", well below
the 120" ceiling. At the slider's bottom (centre 56") the lower finial is
at 43.3", above the 40" wainscot. At the widest point the 4" glass clears
the mirror frame (x 42.5" / 65.5") by 4.25" (west) / 3.25" (east). The
4.75" plate sits above the glass at 78.5-86", beside the narrowing top of
the oval.
